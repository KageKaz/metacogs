When I read things, I like to highlight interesting snippets and save them for later review.[^1] I figure this would be a pretty good place to share and reflect on some of the best of them. These reflections will be denoted with the [[tags/quotes]] tag.
> just to say we must learn
> 
> to bear the pleasures
> 
> as we have borne the pains

**— Nikki Giovanni, [Mothers](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/48228/mothers)**

It kind of reminds me of 
>  I know how to be brought low, and I know how to abound. In any and every circumstance, I have learned the secret of facing plenty and hunger, abundance and need. 

**— Saint Paul, Philippians 4:12** 

So what does it look like to bear pleasure and face plenty? 

I like how "bearing" frames the idea. It requires endurance. Continual effort. 

The effort to remember. To remember the pains. 

And the hedonistic nihilism crouching at your door.

These past few days I've really been enjoying writing for this blog.

I hope I will still remember what it was like to have nothing to write for.
